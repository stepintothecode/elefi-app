Permissions

Every one, and why

EleFi asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and works without any of them.

PermissionWhat forWhen it is asked
RECEIVE_SMSTo record payments from your bank's SMS alerts, on the phone. Messages are never stored. MoreOnly when you turn on Bank SMS, after EleFi explains it.
Notification accessTo record payments from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay and CRED notifications. Other apps' notifications are ignored.Only when you turn on Payment apps; you grant it in Android's settings.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo tell you a payment was recorded, and to remind you of plans and bills due.With Bank SMS or Payment apps, or when you make a plan with a reminder.
CAMERATo photograph a receipt. The photo stays on your phone.Only when you tap Take a photo.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo put plan reminders back after your phone restarts. Nothing else runs at start-up.Granted on install; nothing to ask.
INTERNETOnly to download Smart Ele's language model once, from Hugging Face. None of your data is ever sent.Granted on install; nothing to ask.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo know when the phone is online, so Smart Ele's download starts only then.Granted on install; nothing to ask.

Not asked for

  • Contacts: picking someone from your contacts uses Android's own contact picker, which hands EleFi only the one contact you choose.
  • Photos and files: Android's picker hands EleFi only the photo or file you choose.
  • Location, microphone, accounts: never.
