Questions

Does EleFi upload my data anywhere?

No. EleFi has no server and no account. Your data lives in an encrypted database on your phone. It leaves only when you export a backup file yourself. See the privacy policy.

Why does EleFi want to read SMS?

To record your payments by themselves from your bank's alerts. Only payment alerts are used, on the phone; the message itself is never stored, and OTPs and personal messages are ignored. It's off until you turn it on. See SMS and notifications.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. EleFi never asks for bank logins. It reads the alerts and statements you already get.

What happens if I change phones?

Export a backup from Settings, Backup and Restore, and restore it on the new phone. The file holds everything.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once on Google Play and every feature is yours, including future updates.
