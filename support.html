Help and support

Ask Ele first: she knows the app

Ask me in the app first. I know where everything is, even with no internet.

In EleFi, tap Ask me beside Ele on Home and ask anything: "how do I import a statement?", "why is my balance wrong?", "what does the streak mean?". She takes you to the right place.

Common questions

A payment from SMS wasn't recorded

Check Settings, Automatic capture: Bank SMS is on and allowed. If your bank's message is new to EleFi, use Settings, Teach EleFi, Bank messages, and paste it. For a bank that doesn't send SMS, import its statement.

My balance doesn't match the bank's

Ask Ele "my balance doesn't match". She shows what needs review, what looks recorded twice, and offers to import the statement, which records only what's missing.

A payment was recorded twice

Ask Ele "anything recorded twice?". Open one and delete the copy. Deleted transactions wait in Settings, Deleted, in case you change your mind.

Moving to a new phone

Settings, Backup and Restore, Export: save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. Install EleFi there and use Restore from a file. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it private.

Delete all my data

Settings, Danger, Wipe all data. Or uninstall EleFi: Android removes everything it stored. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Turn off SMS or notification reading

Settings, Automatic capture: switch off Bank SMS or Payment apps. You can also remove the permission in Android's settings.
